These cburnett SVGs are used as a LOCAL FALLBACK only.

By default the board hotlinks chess.com's "Neo" piece theme from their CDN
(https://images.chesscomfiles.com/chess-themes/pieces/neo/...). Those images
are chess.com's proprietary art and are loaded at runtime by the browser. If a
hotlink fails (offline, or chess.com blocks it), the board falls back to the
cburnett SVGs in this folder.

cburnett chess piece SVGs by Colin M.L. Burnett.
